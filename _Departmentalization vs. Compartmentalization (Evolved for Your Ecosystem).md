## **🧩 Departmentalization**

**Departmentalization** is the process of grouping activities, teams, or functions into coherent departments with shared goals.

Key characteristics:

* **activity grouping**: tasks are organized by similarity (e.g., engineering, legal, operations).  
* **goal alignment**: each department has a defined mission.  
* **role clarity**: responsibilities are structured around function.  
* **workflow cohesion**: teams operate within a shared operational logic.

In VaultForge‑Live terms, departmentalization is similar to how you group **public‑safe relics**, **continuity notes**, or **ecosystem declarations** into coherent vault categories.

---

## **🔒 Compartmentalization**

**Compartmentalization** is the division of an organization into smaller, isolated, specialized units — often with restricted access and strict boundaries.

Key characteristics:

* **access restriction**: information is limited to those who need it.  
* **boundary enforcement**: units operate independently to reduce risk.  
* **specialization**: each compartment handles a narrow, protected domain.  
* **containment**: failures or leaks in one area do not compromise others.

In VaultForge‑Live terms, compartmentalization maps directly to your **private vault**, **trade‑secret vault**, and **shadow vault** — each isolated, each sovereign, each with its own continuity chain.
